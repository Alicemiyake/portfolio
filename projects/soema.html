SOEMA

A material passport for speculative design - tracing what a material is made of, where it came from, and where it goes next.

Strategy

At the heart of SOEMA is a simple question: what do we actually know about the materials we design with? SOEMA proposes a passport for every material - a record of origin, composition and afterlife that travels with it from sample to object.

Instead of hiding this information in a spec sheet, SOEMA puts it in the hand: swatches you lift, rotate and read, face to face.

Identity

The identity translates this principle into a graphic language built around the swatch itself - a small, physical unit of information. At the micro scale, each swatch is a single fact: fibre, finish, source. At the macro scale, the pedestal becomes an index, a whole material library held in one object.

Together these elements create a system that moves fluidly between exhibition display, printed documentation and digital interface.

Symbol and Wordmark

The mark reduces the act of turning a swatch over into a single rotating form, echoed in the logotype's tilted, mirrored "S". It stands for inspection - the moment a material is turned toward the light and read.

The wordmark pairs a geometric sans with generous spacing, keeping the tone closer to a lab label than a brand logo.

Visual System

A neutral, near-monochrome base - concrete grey, raw wood, unbleached fabric - keeps focus on the materials themselves. A single warm gold surface marks the "read" side of each swatch, guiding the hand toward the information.

Photography stays close and tactile, documenting texture at the same scale the hand would encounter it.

Outcome

SOEMA reframes material selection as an act of reading rather than guessing. Through identity, exhibition design and documentation, the project sets out a small, physical language for making material provenance legible again.
